# Morning test checklist (about 15 minutes)

Preview: the `hq-rpg` branch address from Vercel (log in to Vercel if it asks; previews are private to you).

## Before Google is connected
1. **Walk around.** WASD or arrows, or the left stick. Shift or RT to run. Click a spot to walk there.
2. **Controller.** Press any button once; a 🎮 chip appears top right. A uses things, B closes, LB/RB change rooms,
   View opens the map, Menu opens the menu, Y opens search.
3. **Honesty check.** Talk to Vesper at the command desk and ask for the briefing. She should say Google isn't set
   up yet, not invent anything.

## Connect Google (docs/SETUP-GOOGLE.md, about 10 minutes)
4. Create the Google project, publish the sign-in screen to Production, add the four Vercel settings, redeploy.
5. Menu → Sign in with Google. You land back in the Command Center with your name in the top bar.

## With your records
6. **Command Center.** Wall monitor and briefing show the latest checkout, loops counts and today's calendar.
   Every card has a link to its original file; open one.
7. **Work District.** One building per lane in your current OS file; the closed lane looks closed. Walk into one
   and open its board, loops and dates.
8. **Coworkers.** Quill in the Library: "Show me recent checkouts". Patch in Comms: "What needs a reply?".
   Gantt in the district: "Which lanes are active?". Each answer says "From your records".
9. **Dictation.** Upstairs → Dream Lounge → the journal desk. Click the text box (or press A on it), then use your
   Superwhisper hotkey. Words land in the box. Saving asks first, and the first save asks to create its own folder.
10. **Voice commands (optional).** Menu → Settings → Voice → turn on. Hold V (or LT), say "Open Hawks War Room",
    let go, confirm.
11. **War Room.** The big screen shows hawkswarroom.com; if the site blocks embedding, use "Open in a new tab".
12. **Personal Space.** Upstairs hall → the private door asks first. Every section stays covered until you reveal it.
13. **Phone.** Open the same link on your phone: you start in the Dream Lounge, laptop rooms are locked on the
    map, and the stick plus A button work. Settings → Phone mode changes that.
14. **Offline.** Turn Wi-Fi off for a moment: the world still walks and panels say you're offline.
